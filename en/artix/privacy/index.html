Privacy Policy for Artix

Effective date: November 10, 2026
Developer: João Souza
Contact: hi@jmosouza.com

Artix is a pass-and-play party game for 3 to 6 players, intended for ages 13 and over. This policy explains what information the app handles. In short: Artix does not collect, send, or share any personal data. Everything the app stores stays on your device.

Information the app stores on your device

Artix saves the following on your device only:

  • Player names. When you type player names on the setup screen, the app saves them so they are filled in the next time you play. They are stored in the app's private preferences storage on your device. They are never sent anywhere.
  • Your settings. The app remembers if you chose to hide the "How to play" screen on start.
  • Decks you have unlocked. The app keeps a list of which art decks you own, so they stay unlocked.

This information stays on your device until you clear it or uninstall Artix. Uninstalling the app deletes all of it. To clear the saved player names without uninstalling, delete the names on the setup screen.

Information the app does not collect

Artix does not:

  • collect your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos, or any other personal information from your account or device;
  • require an account, login, or sign-up;
  • use analytics, advertising identifiers, advertising networks, or tracking of any kind;
  • use crash-reporting or diagnostics services that send data to the developer;
  • access your camera, microphone, location, contacts, storage, or files;
  • send any data over the internet. The released app has no network access and makes no network requests.

The only device feature Artix uses is haptic feedback (vibration on taps and confirmations) and keeping the screen on during play. Neither sends any information out.

Storage location and security

All app data is stored locally in the app's private storage on the device. Other apps cannot read it under normal conditions. Data is not transferred to the developer, to a server, or to any cloud service.

Third parties

Artix does not share data with any third party because it does not transfer data off your device. The app uses open-source Flutter software components that run on your device and do not receive your data. Google Play handles the app download and installation under Google's own privacy policy.

Children's privacy

Artix is not directed at children. Some of the artworks in the app contain mild nudity, so the app is intended for players aged 13 and over. Artix does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. The only information entered in the app is the player names you choose to type, which stay on your device. If you believe a child has entered personal information in the app, contact us at hi@jmosouza.com and we will help you remove it from the device.

Your rights

Because Artix does not collect or transmit personal data, the developer holds no data about you to access, correct, export, or delete. You control everything the app stores on your device. You can delete it at any time by clearing names in the app or uninstalling Artix. If you have questions about this policy, contact hi@jmosouza.com.

Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under local law, such as the EU/UK GDPR or Brazil's LGPD. Because the app holds no personal data on our side, there is nothing for us to provide, but you may contact us at hi@jmosouza.com with any question.

Changes to this policy

If Artix changes what information it handles, this policy will be updated and the effective date above will change. If a change adds data collection, we will say so clearly in this policy and in the app's store listing before the change is released. Please check this page from time to time.
